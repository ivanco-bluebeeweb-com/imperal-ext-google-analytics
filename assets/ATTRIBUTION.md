# Google Analytics brand asset status

`icon.svg` is a neutral development placeholder. It is **not** an official Google Analytics logo and must not be used in a released build.

Before release, replace it with the official Google Analytics product icon obtained from Google's current brand-assets source, preserve its original geometry and colours, and record here:

- official source URL;
- retrieval date;
- downloaded filename and checksum;
- confirmation that the asset was reviewed against the current Google brand guidance.

Do not substitute a logo from a third-party icon library or recreate it manually.
